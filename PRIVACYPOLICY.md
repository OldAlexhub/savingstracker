## 1. Overview

Savings Tracker is a free Android app with advertising. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information. Please read it carefully.

**Summary:** Your savings and debt data stays on your device. The app uses Google AdMob to serve ads, which may involve data processing by Google and its partners.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2a. Information You Enter (Stored Locally Only)

All data you enter in the app — including savings goals, contribution amounts, debt account names, balances, APR, payment history, and notes — is stored **locally on your device only** using Android's secure local storage (AsyncStorage).

- We do **not** transmit your financial data to any server.
- We do **not** back up your financial data to any cloud service.
- We do **not** have access to your financial data at any time.
- No account or login is required.

### 2b. Information Collected by Advertising Services

This app is **free with ads** supported by Google AdMob. By using this app, you agree that advertising services may process certain data as described below.

**Google AdMob** and its partners may collect and process:
- Device identifiers (Android Advertising ID, device model, OS version)
- App interactions and usage events related to ads
- Approximate location (based on IP address, if permitted)
- Diagnostics (ad loading performance, crash data related to ads)
- App and ad performance metrics

This data is used to serve relevant ads and measure ad effectiveness. We do not control what data Google AdMob collects or how it is processed. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for full details:
[https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

**We do not sell your data.** We do not receive personal information from AdMob. We only receive aggregate performance reports.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal financial data with any third party.

The only data sharing that occurs is through Google AdMob's advertising SDK, which operates under Google's own privacy policies and may share aggregated or pseudonymous advertising data with Google's advertising partners.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

### Your Financial Data
All your savings and debt data is stored on your device. You can delete it at any time:
- Open the app → Settings → "Clear All Data"
- Or uninstall the app (all local data is removed by Android on uninstall)

We cannot delete your data because we do not have it — it lives only on your device.

### Advertising Data
To opt out of personalized advertising or request deletion of advertising data collected by Google:
- Open your device Settings → Privacy → Ads → "Delete advertising ID"
- Visit [https://adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com)
- Visit [https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy](https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy)

## 8. Security

Your financial data is stored locally on your Android device using AsyncStorage, which is protected by Android's application sandbox. Only the Savings Tracker app can access its own storage.

We recommend:
- Using a device PIN, pattern, or biometric lock
- Not sharing your device with untrusted parties
- Backing up your phone using Android's built-in backup features if desired
