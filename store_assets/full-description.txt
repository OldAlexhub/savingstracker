Savings Tracker helps you plan savings goals, compare saving strategies, track debts manually, and explore debt payoff methods — all on your device, with no login or bank connection required.

SAVINGS GOALS
• Create and track multiple savings goals (vacation, emergency fund, new car, etc.)
• Choose from 10 educational saving strategies
• Log contributions and see how your balance grows over time
• View estimated completion dates based on your entries
• Try the 52-Week Savings Challenge or the Reverse 52-Week Challenge
• Archive or mark goals complete when you reach them

SAVINGS STRATEGIES (EDUCATIONAL PLANNING METHODS)
• Fixed Weekly Saving
• Fixed Monthly Saving
• Pay Yourself First Planner
• Emergency Fund Builder
• Sinking Fund Planner
• 52-Week Savings Challenge
• Reverse 52-Week Savings Challenge
• Envelope-Style Goal Buckets
• No-Spend Day Savings Tracker
• Custom Savings Plan

DEBT TRACKER
• Add any type of debt: credit cards, student loans, auto loans, mortgages, personal loans, medical bills
• Track current balance, original balance, APR, and minimum payment manually
• Log payments and see your balance decrease over time
• View estimated payoff dates based on your entries

DEBT PAYOFF STRATEGIES (EDUCATIONAL COMPARISON)
• Debt Avalanche – highest APR first (estimated lowest total interest)
• Debt Snowball – smallest balance first (estimated quick wins)
• Hybrid – one quick win first, then by APR
• Equal Extra Payment – split extra payments across all debts
• Custom Order – choose your own payoff sequence

IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER
Savings Tracker is an educational planning and tracking tool, not financial advice. All calculations are estimates based on data you enter manually. Results are labeled "estimated" throughout the app. Always consult a qualified financial professional before making financial decisions.

This app does not connect to banks, move money, pull credit scores, or claim guaranteed financial results.
