PRIVACY SUMMARY — Savings Tracker by Old Alex Hub

REQUIRED ANONYMOUS SYNC
- The app creates a random installation ID; no account, email, or phone number is requested.
- Savings goals, debts, balances, APRs, dates, contribution/payment histories, names, and notes are uploaded to tracking.server.oldalexhub.com.
- Names and notes are sent unchanged. Users should not enter personal or account information in free text.
- Manufacturer/model, Android and app versions, locale, timezone, screen class, capability flags, app activity, and diagnostics are collected.
- The app does not collect contacts, precise GPS, IMEI, hardware serial number, or first-party access to the Advertising ID.

SECURITY AND RETENTION
- Local data is kept in an encrypted MMKV envelope; keys and the anonymous token are protected by Android Keystore.
- Data is encrypted in transit. Installation bearer tokens are hashed on the server.
- Finance snapshots expire after deletion or 24 months of inactivity; raw analytics/device history after 13 months; admin audits after 24 months.

DELETION
- Settings > Delete local and server data purges the current installation’s server records and local data.
- Uninstalling removes local data but cannot send a server deletion request, so use the in-app action first.
- There is no cloud restore or cross-device recovery.

ADVERTISING
- Google AdMob and its partners may process identifiers, ad activity, diagnostics, and approximate network-derived location.
- Google UMP presents legally required regional choices. Core tracking and baseline insights remain available with limited/non-personalized ads.
