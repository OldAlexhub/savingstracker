# Feature Graphic Design Notes — Savings Tracker

## Specifications
- Size: 1024 x 500 px (required by Google Play)
- Format: PNG or JPEG
- No rounded corners (Play Store applies them)
- No transparency

## Recommended Design

### Background
- Gradient: `#1B5E20` (top-left) to `#2E7D32` (bottom-right)
- OR solid `#2E7D32` with a subtle pattern overlay

### Left Side (60% of width)
- App icon (circle, 160px)
- App name: "Savings Tracker" in white bold font, 64-72pt
- Tagline: "Plan. Track. Progress." in white/light green, 28-32pt
- Developer: "by Old Alex Hub" in light green, 18pt

### Right Side (40% of width)
- Mockup showing dashboard screen with:
  - Total Saved card: "$2,450"
  - Progress bar filled to 65%
  - Active Goals: 3
  - Debt Paid: $800

### Color Palette
- Primary: `#2E7D32` (dark green)
- Accent: `#4CAF50` (light green)
- Text: `#FFFFFF` (white)
- Highlight: `#F57F17` (amber)

### Typography
- App name font: Bold, sans-serif (Roboto Bold or similar)
- Tagline: Regular weight, slightly transparent white
- No decorative fonts

### Legal Notes
- Do NOT include any dollar amounts that imply guaranteed results
- Do NOT include phrases like "Save more money!" which could be misleading
- OK to show a sample dashboard with fictional data
- Feature graphic must comply with Google Play guidelines

## Tools
- Adobe Illustrator or Figma recommended
- Free alternative: Canva (use the 1024×500 custom size)

## File to deliver
- `feature-graphic.png` (place in `store_assets/` folder once created)
